How it works

The app connects your Reachy Mini to ElevenLabs Conversational AI, enabling natural voice interactions. Simply speak to your robot and it will respond with expressive, human-like speech.

The system uses advanced audio processing to convert the robot's stereo microphone input to the format expected by ElevenLabs, ensuring crystal-clear voice recognition.

During conversations, the robot's head wobbles naturally in sync with the audio output, creating an engaging and lifelike interaction experience. The head movements are generated in real-time based on the speech audio waveform.

Powered by cutting-edge AI from ElevenLabs, with open-source development by mindmodel.ai and ageai.io.

How to install

  1. Make sure you have the Reachy Mini Control app installed.
  2. Connect to your Reachy Mini using the Control app.
  3. Navigate to the "Applications" tab.
  4. Find "ElevenLabs Conversation" in the list.
  5. Click "Install" and wait for installation to complete.
  6. Start the app and configure your ElevenLabs Agent ID at http://localhost:7861/
